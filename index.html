About Us

Compliance expertise since 1997 — now AI-powered.

ISOXPERT grew out of a multi-disciplinary management & IT consultancy founded in 1997 in Karachi, Pakistan. For more than two decades we've guided manufacturers, exporters and service organisations through certification and compliance — work that demanded precision, deep standards knowledge and a genuine partnership with every client.

That hands-on heritage spans ISO 9001, 14001, 27001 and 22000, social-accountability standards like SA 8000, WRAP and BSCI, supply-chain security frameworks such as C-TPAT and BRC, product certification including CE marking, and systems digitisation with QuickBooks and Sage ACT!.

Today we put that same consulting expertise into an AI-powered platform — so compliance is faster, smarter and audit-ready for every organisation.

ISOXPERT is not a certification body and does not issue certificates. We prepare organisations for certification and coordinate local audit delivery — certification audits and decisions always rest with independent accredited certification bodies. ISOXPERT is under common ownership with Company Certification International (CCI), a certification body accredited by IAR (IAR-112), USA — a relationship we disclose in full in our governance; engaging ISOXPERT confers no advantage in any certification decision, CCI's included. Learn about our Certification Body Partnership Program.

FAQ

Common questions

What ISOXPERT is, the standards we cover, and how certification works.

What is ISOXPERT?

ISOXPERT is an AI-powered ecosystem of four connected apps — Compliance360, Inspection360, QC360 and Audit360 — for governance, risk, compliance, inspection, quality control and certification audits. It is built by an independent Pakistani team with management-systems consulting and audit experience since 1997.

Which ISO standards does ISOXPERT support?

ISOXPERT supports the leading ISO management system standards including ISO 9001 (quality), ISO 14001 (environment), ISO 45001 (health & safety), ISO 27001 (information security), ISO 22000 (food safety) and ISO 22301 (business continuity), plus social-accountability, supply-chain and product certification schemes.

Who issues the ISO certificate?

Certification audits and certification decisions are always carried out by independent accredited certification bodies. ISOXPERT is not a certification body and does not issue certificates — we prepare your organisation, digitise your management system and coordinate the audit process so certification runs smoothly. ISOXPERT is under common ownership with Company Certification International (CCI), a certification body accredited by IAR (IAR-112), USA; the relationship is disclosed in our governance statement, you are free to certify with any body you choose, and engaging ISOXPERT confers no advantage in any certification decision.

Can ISOXPERT help us get ISO certified?

Yes. The ISOXPERT platform prepares you end-to-end — generating clause-mapped documentation, running gap analysis, managing audit evidence and keeping you audit-ready between surveillance visits — while your certification audit and decision are carried out by an independent accredited certification body.
