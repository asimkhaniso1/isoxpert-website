How it works

From Enquiry to Manufacturable Specification

Traditional custom manufacturing often starts with emails, drawings, WhatsApp messages and multiple rounds of clarification. Design360 creates a structured digital journey — customers can explore what's possible while your sales and production teams receive clearer, structured requirements.

Configure→ Visualize→ Validate→ Specify→ Quote→ Sample→ Manufacture
Capabilities

What Design360 Can Do

Interactive Product Configurator

Let customers select dimensions, materials, colours, constructions, finishes, quantities and other product-specific parameters.

Live Product Visualization

Show customers how their selections affect the finished product through interactive 2D or 3D visualization.

Artwork & Branding

Allow customers to upload logos, artwork, patterns, monograms and other branding elements directly into the design workflow.

AI Design Review

Analyse customer selections and artwork and provide intelligent recommendations before submission.

Manufacturability Validation

Apply your own production rules, limitations and capabilities to identify potential manufacturing issues before quotation or sampling.

Smart Specifications

Automatically convert customer selections into structured product specifications for sales, engineering and production teams.

RFQ & Sample Requests

Turn completed designs directly into quotation, sample or prototype requests with all relevant specifications attached.

Indicative Costing & MOQ

Where appropriate, provide indicative pricing, minimum-order quantities and lead-time guidance based on configuration.

FAQ

Design360 questions

What is Design360?

Design360 turns a traditional manufacturing website into an interactive Design Studio, letting customers configure products, visualize options, upload artwork, validate requirements and submit production-ready specifications or RFQs.

Is Design360 a generic off-the-shelf configurator?

No. We model your products, manufacturing capabilities, design constraints, terminology and commercial workflow to create a Design Studio specific to your business.

Which industries can use Design360?

Textiles, elastic, webbing and narrow fabrics; labels, ribbons and garment accessories; packaging and printing; chocolates and custom food products; promotional and branded products; metal and fabricated components; rubber, silicone and moulded products; and other configure-to-order manufacturing.

Can Design360 be white-labelled under our own brand?

Yes. Design360 can power a fully branded experience integrated directly into your existing website — your customers see your brand, not another software platform.

Does Design360 integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. Design360 can integrate with your website, CRM, ERP, inventory, production, email, WhatsApp, RFQ, quotation, sample approval and quality control processes.
