Auditor network

A vetted network — approved by the certification body, not by us.

We maintain a network of associate auditors across Pakistan's major industrial centres. For certification work, every auditor signs an individual agreement with the partner CB, is competence-evaluated and approved by the CB, and is witness-audited before independent assignment.

ISO 9001 — Quality ISO 14001 — Environment ISO 45001 — OH&S ISO/IEC 27001 — Information Security ISO/IEC 20000-1 — IT Service Management ISO 22000 — Food Safety

Competence, evidenced

CVs, qualifications, audit logs and scheme registrations (IRCA/CQI where held) are provided to the partner CB for its own evaluation under ISO/IEC 17021-1 clause 7 — we never self-certify competence.

Independence, per assignment

Every auditor signs impartiality and conflict-of-interest declarations for each assignment. Anyone who has provided consultancy to a client is excluded from that client's audit under the two-year rule.

Continuing development

Scheme-update briefings, calibration sessions and CPD tracking keep the network current — with records available to the CB's competence-management process.

Delivery process

From enquiry to decision — with the CB in control at every gate.

1 · Application

We prepare the client's application and scope details in the CB's format; the CB performs application review and audit-time calculation.

2 · Team appointment

The CB appoints the audit team from its approved pool. We handle scheduling, logistics and client liaison.

3 · Audit delivery

Stage 1 and Stage 2 audits delivered on-site (or ICT-assisted where the scheme permits), with time-stamped evidence and complete working papers.

4 · Evidence pack

The full audit pack flows to the CB through our audit-management platform — nothing summarised, nothing withheld.

5 · Review & decision

Technical review and the certification decision are performed by the CB, by personnel independent of the audit — always.

6 · Surveillance cycle

We manage the local surveillance calendar and client relationship through the three-year cycle; the CB controls the audit programme.

Infrastructure

Oversight from 4,000 km away — made easy.

Our audit-management platform gives partner CBs live visibility of schedules, evidence, working papers and timelines — supporting remote technical review, witness planning and unannounced spot checks. Technology supports the process; it never makes certification judgements.
