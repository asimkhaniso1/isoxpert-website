Disclosed before you ask

Our group includes a certification body.

ISOXPERT is under common ownership with Company Certification International (CCI), a certification body accredited by International Accreditation Registrar (IAR), USA — accreditation No. IAR-112 — which provides its own certification services in Pakistan. CCI additionally delivers internationally recognized (MRA) certification through an established arrangement with an accredited international certification body: CCI performs commercial and coordination functions, while that body retains all certification authority — the certification agreement, the certification decision and certificate issuance in its own name. The arrangement is disclosed in full during due diligence. Our Compliance360 and Inspection360 platforms are ISOXPERT products. We put this on the first page because your assessors will find it, and we would rather you read it from us. The structure we propose is designed so this relationship sits inside your impartiality analysis from day one: full disclosure, a shared conflict-of-interest register, route-separation safeguards, and no compensation contingent on any certification outcome. Compliance architecture

Built to pass your accreditation body's scrutiny.

Dedicated entity

The representation agreement is executed by a dedicated, single-purpose Pakistani company that performs no consultancy, sells no software and issues no certificates — a clean "entity operating on behalf of the CB" under IAF MD 23.

Auditors contract with you

Every auditor signs an individual agreement with the CB, is competence-approved by the CB, and is witness-audited during the pilot. We coordinate; you appoint.

Consultancy firewall

Our consulting and software activities — and our group's certification body, Company Certification International (CCI), including its existing arrangement with an accredited international certification body — are disclosed up-front and firewalled. A shared conflict-of-interest register is visible to you, and the two-year rule of ISO/IEC 17021-1 is enforced per assignment.

No decision pressure

Our compensation is never contingent on a positive certification decision — we are paid for services rendered whether or not certification is granted.

Complaints route to you

All complaints and appeals are forwarded to your documented process within 48 hours. Certification disputes are never resolved locally.

Oversight made easy

Audit evidence, working papers and time-stamped records flow through our audit-management platform — so technical review and unannounced spot checks work from anywhere.

FAQ

Questions certification bodies ask us

Is ISOXPERT a certification body?

No. ISOXPERT is not a certification body and does not issue certificates. We provide Pakistan market development, client relationship management and local audit-delivery coordination for one accredited international Certification Body partner. All certification functions — auditor competence approval, audit programme control, technical review, certification decisions, certificate issuance and use of accreditation marks — are retained by the CB. Our group does, however, include a certification body — Company Certification International (CCI), accredited by IAR (IAR-112), USA, under common ownership with ISOXPERT; CCI also delivers internationally recognized (MRA) certification through an established arrangement with an accredited international certification body. These relationships are disclosed up-front and sit inside the partner CB's impartiality analysis.

What partnership structure do you propose?

A representation / local-office agreement structured to satisfy IAF MD 23 (Control of Entities Operating on Behalf of Accredited Certification Bodies), executed by a dedicated single-purpose Pakistani entity that performs no consultancy. Auditors contract individually with the CB and are approved, appointed and witness-audited by the CB.

How is impartiality protected?

The contracting entity performs no consultancy and issues no certificates. Related consulting and software activities — and the group's certification body, Company Certification International (CCI) — are disclosed and firewalled, with a shared conflict-of-interest register visible to the CB. Personnel who have provided consultancy to a client are excluded from that client's audit in line with the two-year rule of ISO/IEC 17021-1, and all complaints and appeals route to the CB's documented process.

Which schemes are the priority for Pakistan?

ISO/IEC 27001 for the IT and IT-export sector, ISO/IEC 20000-1 IT service management, and ISO 22000 food safety for processors and exporters — alongside ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001.

What does the commercial model look like?

A published fee schedule for the functions only the CB performs — application review, technical review and certification decision, certificate issuance and registry listing, and annual maintenance — with local delivery economics on our side. We discuss specifics after a first conversation and mutual NDA. We do not propose royalty or per-certificate arrangements.
